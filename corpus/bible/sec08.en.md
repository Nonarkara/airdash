# 8. The Action Framework

No one on AirDash is ever shown a passive label. Every band ships with exactly one verb, in both languages, sized to dominate the page — because under stress people pattern-match rather than read, and a verb is the fastest pattern there is. This chapter defines the verbs and the checklists that back them.

## 8.1 One band, one verb

| Band | Thai verb | English verb | For a person, this means |
|---|---|---|---|
| normal | อากาศดี | GOOD AIR | live normally; a fine day for outdoor exercise |
| watch | ติดตามสถานการณ์ | STAY INFORMED | check again before long outdoor plans; sensitive groups pay attention |
| elevated | ลดกิจกรรมกลางแจ้ง | LIMIT OUTDOOR TIME | shorten and lighten outdoor activity; masks for sensitive groups |
| high | ป้องกันทันที | PROTECT NOW | stay indoors, seal the room, mask up if you must go out |

During the dust season a "normal" national reading renders as **LOW — STAY INFORMED**, never as an all-clear: a clean morning in the burning months is a pause, not an ending.

## 8.2 Checklists for citizens

- **Masks.** A certified respirator (N95 or equivalent) filters fine particles; a loose surgical or cloth mask mostly does not. Fit matters as much as the filter — air that goes around the mask is unfiltered air.
- **The clean room.** Pick one room, keep windows and doors shut, run an air purifier with a HEPA filter (or a well-built DIY fan-and-filter box), and let the household ride out the worst hours there. Cleaning one room is achievable; cleaning a whole house usually is not.
- **Sensitive groups first.** Children breathe faster and lower to the ground; the elderly, pregnant women, and anyone with heart or lung disease feel effects at levels healthy adults shrug off. Their thresholds are one band stricter than everyone else's.
- **Time your outdoor life.** Air is typically worst in the calm early morning and best after wind picks up or rain falls — the washout panel exists precisely to time errands and exercise around forecast relief.

## 8.3 Checklists for municipal operators

- Move school assemblies, sports days, and outdoor classes indoors when a district goes elevated; do not wait for the national picture.
- Apply outdoor-work rules — rotation, breaks, respirators — for street sweepers, traffic police, and construction crews.
- Enforce burning restrictions early in the season while ventilation is still good; a ban announced during the peak is a ban announced too late.
- Open designated clean rooms in public buildings and publicize their locations; a purifier in a district office nobody knows about protects nobody.
- Use the washout outlook honestly: forecast rain is a reason to schedule, never a reason to relax enforcement.

## 8.4 Hotlines and official channels

AirDash prioritises attention; it does not issue orders. The buttons that matter are one tap away throughout the citizen panel: **1650** (PCD pollution hotline), **1422** (Department of Disease Control health line), **1669** (emergency medical services), plus the official portals pcd.go.th and air4thai.pcd.go.th. When AirDash and an official advisory disagree, follow the official advisory — and tell us, so we can find the bug.
