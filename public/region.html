From raw data to preparation

  1. Raw: check provider, units, observation time and averaging period.
  2. Checked: separate stale readings and unconnected countries; never fill gaps with zero.
  3. Cooked: AOD is column aerosol; fires are signals, not arriving surface concentration. This page does not calculate transport paths or arrival times.
  4. Act: check destination readings before travel, prepare a cleaner-air room and follow local exposure guidance.

Regional flood evidence

Raw: GDACS episodes and report periods → checked: country, timestamps and a six-hour retrieval age limit → cooked: event locations, without inventing inundation boundaries → action: check gauges and local warnings before travelling or relocating.

Reports cover the last 14 days, not current inundation. GDACS alert colours describe humanitarian impact, not road safety or water depth. FloodDash assessments cover Thailand; MRC and ADINet are references. Yesterday’s satellite rain is not a flood forecast.
