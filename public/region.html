From raw data to preparation

  1. Raw: check provider, units, observation time and averaging period.
  2. Checked: separate stale readings and unconnected countries; never fill gaps with zero.
  3. Cooked: AOD is column aerosol; fires are signals, not arriving surface concentration. This page does not calculate transport paths or arrival times.
  4. Act: check destination readings before travel, prepare a cleaner-air room and follow local exposure guidance.
