## Corrections to the brief (important)
1. **Thai PM2.5 24-h standard 37.5 ug/m3 took effect 1 June 2023, not 1 Jan 2023.** Read the Royal Gazette notification (Vol. 139 Special Part 163 Ng, 8 July 2022): 50 ug/m3 until 31 May 2023, 37.5 from 1 June 2023; annual 15 ug/m3 in force from 9 July 2022. (`nec2022pm25std`). The Thai AQI 2023 notification (dated 16 May 2023, Gazette 3 July 2023; PM2.5 bands 0-15, 15.1-25, 25.1-37.5, 37.6-75, 75.1+) is `pcd2023aqi`. A "1 Jan 2023" date is not supported by any source I read.
2. **"ASEAN Cooperation Plan 2023-2030" is really the "Second Roadmap on ASEAN Cooperation towards Transboundary Haze Pollution Control with Means of Implementation (Haze-free Roadmap) 2023-2030"**, adopted at COP-18, Vientiane, 23 Aug 2023 (`haze_roadmap2`). First roadmap 2016-2020 adopted at COP-12, 11 Aug 2016 (`haze_roadmap1`).
3. **Quah 2002**: title is "Transboundary Pollution in Southeast Asia: The Indonesian Fires" (World Development 30(3):429-441), not "Transboundary haze".
4. **CAMS PM2.5 "underestimation in Asia" could NOT be confirmed.** Ali et al. 2022 (China, 1,675 stations) report CAMS significantly OVER-estimates PM2.5/PM10 over most regions (under-estimates only at the cleanest sites). Tan 2023 (Malaysia) reports weak skill (CSI 31%, R2 0.36), Wu 2020 (China) RMSE 38-83 ug/m3, Bran 2022 shows a WRF-Chem model (not CAMS) under-estimating northern-Thailand PM2.5 by ~24-27%, Marvin 2024 shows GEOS-Chem under-estimating SE Asian burning-season PM by up to 54%. Do not write "CAMS underestimates" without new evidence.

- Ratification/approval -> deposit (ASEAN Haze Portal table, as of 20 Jan 2015): Thailand 10 Sep 2003 -> 26 Sep 2003; Indonesia 14 Oct 2014 -> 20 Jan 2015 (Indonesian parliament voted 16 Sep 2014, Law No. 26/2014); Myanmar 5 Mar 2003 -> 17 Mar 2003. All 10 ASEAN members are parties.
